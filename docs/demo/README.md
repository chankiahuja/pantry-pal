# Pantry Pal staging demo

[Watch or download the narrated demo](pantrypal-staging-demo.mp4) · [Captions (SRT)](captions.srt)

**Duration:** 3 minutes 32 seconds. **Captured:** 21 November 2026.

The video combines real screen captures from the staging application with generated English narration. It is an edited walkthrough, rather than a continuous cursor recording. Sign-in, email addresses and OTP entry are omitted.

## What it shows

- Ten sample inventory items and overlapping stock/freshness conditions.
- Alert priority: Expired → Out of Stock → Expiring Soon → Low Stock.
- The bell's unread number and the amber dot for items that still need attention after being read.
- Finding rice, consuming 0.5 kg, and using Undo to restore its original quantity.
- Reviewing shortages, creating a shopping list, renaming it and inspecting linked units.
- Marking an item purchased and completing the purchase to restock linked inventory.
- Compare Prices, including Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart and Local Store sample cards, plus the fixed sample basket.

## Scope and limits

Email reminders for items needing attention are planned for a future release; the demonstrated reminders are in-app. Price comparisons are illustrative figures for a fixed basket, not live retailer quotes for the selected shopping list. Delivery charges and offers are excluded.

Production remains deferred. The footage demonstrates these particular staging interactions; it is not evidence that every acceptance criterion, browser or database scenario has passed. Account deletion, actual feedback delivery, the OTP exchange and mobile layouts are outside this walkthrough. Repeating purchase completion is not demonstrated.

After capture, changed inventory quantities were restored. A completed demo shopping list and adjustment history remained in the demo account. Expiry examples reflect the capture date and may differ when staging is viewed later.

The MP4 includes narration. Load `captions.srt` in a video player for optional text captions; concise captions are also shown inside the video. If GitHub's file viewer does not play the video inline, select **View raw** or download the MP4 and open it in a video player.
